How we work

From the source to a supported conclusion.

Understanding the code is the beginning. Making it useful to your case is the work. What you receive is the end product: claim charts that cite the code, line by line.

Step 1 · The details

Ingest

We examine the source code and document the key components, behaviors, and features of the system.

Step 2 · The big picture

Digest

We connect those details into a clear, high-level view of the architecture: how the system works and how its parts fit together.

Step 3 · The evidence

Synthesize

We map documented features to claim elements, building claim charts with precise citations and technical support for your expert report.
